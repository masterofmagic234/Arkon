## Presentation

- Four authored visual identities: bronze entrance portal with moon crest and
  oak-anchored festoon lights; cyan moon-garden lamps, extruded crescent/orrery
  sculpture in the physically closed pond and luminous lotus ornaments;
  lavender gallery lamps, a rose medallion on the existing tall stone arch,
  ivy and wisteria; warm golden-oak festoons attached to its actual branch
  anchors. Pavilion gains roof seams, eave inlay and lantern strings.
- Blue-grey staggered stone setts replace the plain gravel appearance using
  the same single texture sample. Small flush coloured edge inlays guide the
  route. Existing gravel-footstep classification and physical route remain.
- Flowers gain cupped petals and a palette per zone. Fireflies have smaller,
  softer pearl/mint/gold cores instead of large green blobs.
- New architecture is generated by scripts/level1_night_garden.gd. It is
  immutable, spatially batched into 42 surfaces, 32,824 triangles and two
  shared materials. No new real-time lights, viewports, textures or post passes.
  Existing lamp colours change; the existing 26-light count is preserved.
- Entrance posts and supply-case footprints use the existing single Props
  source for rendering placements, physics and navigation. The player's full
  1.8m capsule crosses the new entrance. Pond ornaments stay on closed water.

## Gameplay

- scripts/level1_encounters.gd owns nine additional pre-existing guards in
  four finite groups: 2 after Door01, 3 after Door02, 2 after Door03, 2 after
  all six acorns/three gates are recovered. The original five squirrels stay.
  Guards combine scouts, throwers and one tank. Original health/damage/speed,
  weapon cadence and attack telegraphs are unchanged. No forced kill objective.
- Guards are authored before the first frame, included in the minimap and
  hittable. They start dormant, show a 1.8s gate warning, then gain a further
  1s attack cooldown. Repeated gate commands cannot duplicate or restart them.
  Pause freezes warnings. Failure/boarding stops the encounter controller.
  No spawning behind the player, no respawns, no inflated health values.
- Custom patrol_cells preserve each guard's local area. Reinforcements stop
  simulation beyond 32m; their meshes stop rendering at 30m and cast no shadows.
  Existing five enemy defaults are unchanged.
- Three optional teal/brass supply cases at cells (6,5), (44,5), (33,32):
  +6 charges and +15 health, capped at the original maxima, collected by the
  actual Area3D once only. No mission progress is granted. The earlier secret
  gardener chest remains. HUD adds guard/secret tallies below the acorn count.
- Campaign stays menu -> Level 1 -> Level 2 -> Level 3 -> menu. No Level 2/3
  implementation, controls, assets or race tuning changed.

## Validation and limits

- Thirteen headless gates: the existing eleven fixtures, plus the new
  tools/level1_garden_test.gd and --explore mode of level1_playthrough.gd.
  Both new gates are in the Android workflow before export. Keep Ubuntu 24.04,
  Godot 4.7.2, modern Mesa native gate and existing workflow configuration.
- Normal and exploration routes drive the production player using inputs,
  legal shots, real pickups/gate triggers and actual car boarding. No actor
  teleports or health/ammo/progress overrides in those complete routes. The
  initial verified runs used 31 shots: 49.0 simulated seconds with no supplies,
  64.1s visiting all three supplies. These are perfect-aim automated drivers,
  not human completion times or difficulty ratings.
- New behavioral fixture covers real-gate warning/activation, 2s pause,
  duplicate commands, actual supply approaches/caps/re-entry, a new sentry's
  warned projectile dealing 8 damage, every patrol endpoint's navigation,
  entrance capsule clearance, static art budgets and defeat shutdown.
- Thirteen native Compatibility poses were inspected alongside the same
  starting poses. New draw submissions/geometry are bounded but are added
  work; do not claim unchanged or improved phone FPS. Mesa llvmpipe is a
  software renderer. Phone frame rate, touch feel and user art acceptance
  remain unmeasured.
- Export stays arm64 debug, package com.acornhunter.carolina, existing filename
  build/AcornHunter-MoonlightSprint.apk, now version 1.16 / code 17. Deliver
