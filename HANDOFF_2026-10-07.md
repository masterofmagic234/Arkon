## Ferrari Grand Prix Challenge NES tribute

Level2 is also intended as a tribute to Ferrari Grand Prix Challenge NES.

Research conclusion:
- available material supports digital Left/Right steering and an arcade-style response rather than simulation.
- steering/front-wheel response is visually important.
- excessive speed in corners can lead to speed loss / leaving the track.
- exact original source/ROM implementation was not found; do not claim source-level equivalence.

Default/tribute mode should keep a NES-like steering response. NFS-style mode can later be a separate handling/camera profile over the same pseudo-3D renderer.
