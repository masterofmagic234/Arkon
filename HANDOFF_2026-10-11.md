## Second pass

- Four approved wall_zone textures are lit world-space masonry materials,
  alongside new original 1024 px limestone. Wall caps, damp bases, gate posts
  and the arch have matching stone detail. Texture prompt/provenance is in
  assets/level1/textures/README.md; its JPEG uses mipmaps and desktop/Android
  VRAM compression. Original authored map and campaign layout are unchanged.
- Pond water is a smooth oval clipped INSIDE the exact physical blocked-cell
  contour with an inward shore margin. It has fine animated wave normals,
  matching sky/moon reflection, four nearby lantern reflections, a wet bank,
  stones, swaying reeds and lily pads. Shore-distance interpolation is sampled
  on subdivided triangles, avoiding the earlier internal triangular seams.
  Original spatial water sound is retained and anchored at the same centre.
- 26 illuminated lanterns, 10 oriented benches and 294 independently fluttering,
  pulsing fireflies. The latter use chunked additive billboard MultiMeshes;
  Compatibility's unshaded pass needs their color in ALBEDO. No particle lights
  or per-frame CPU particle-position updates. Main wall-top bushes are unchanged.
- One shared set of 38 scenery footprints drives visual placements, physics,
  grass exclusion and navigation. Recast bakes floor/obstruction CPU geometry
  asynchronously with a 0.45 m body margin; enemies use 0.10 m waypoint tolerance
  so their capsules do not cut concave wall/bench corners. Initial readiness
  checks the region, map synchronization and an actual query surface.
- Ground is divided into 90 patches and gravel into 25 sections, sharing world
  texture coordinates. Actual light-range/AABB checks find at most five lanterns
  per ground patch, below Compatibility's eight-light object limit. Scenery
  batches are spatially chunked at 12 m. Grass stays off gravel and prop legs.
- Fixed reproduced bugs: thieves carrying stone pickup pedestals; focus loss
  retaining touch movement/fire; release/cancel outside controls sticking inputs;
  old shot-feedback timers clearing a fresh marker; active gameplay input and
  a captured cursor over defeat results; overlapping zone banner transitions.
  Pedestals stay on the ground; focus loss clears inputs and pauses; result
  panels retain a usable desktop cursor across pause/resume.

## Park and campaign

- Four connected park episodes: entrance oak, moonlit pond/gazebo, overgrown
  stone courtyard, golden oak and the parked original 240SX.
- Authored 48 x 36 grid, 1.8 m cells, 585 blocked cells. Three actual locked
  bottlenecks prevent skipping the corresponding key. Winding gravel ribbon,
  hedges, trees, benches, signs, hanging lanterns, fireflies and an optional
  one-use healing/ammo chest.
- `tools/generate_level1_layout.py` regenerates LevelData, actor positions and
  the collision scene deterministically. Python is an offline authoring tool.
- Environment uses chunked MultiMeshes, shared resources, actual lit grass,
  generated original ground texture, animated pond, blue night fog and warm
  local lights. Stone/wood have lit surface grain and lichen rather than flat
  fills. Collisions/navigation use the same authored grid.
- 6 acorns and 3 keys. All gates must open. The final pickup preserves player
  control; entering ExitCar/BoardingArea fades out and emits the existing
  campaign completion fact. SceneFlow transitions safely outside physics flush.
- The parked car reuses Race240SXRig with duplicated materials. Level 2 source
  geometry/materials and driving logic are retained.

## Actors, input and sound

- All five archetypes use the existing shared rigged squirrel. Tank armour,
  thief satchel and runner scarf help distinguish them. Physical capsules,
  navigation routes, throttled perception and real line of sight.
- Important navigation fix: mesh paths are on the ground, actor origins at
  waist height. `path_height_offset = -ground_y` aligns reach checks without
  making waypoint tolerance large enough to cut corners.
- Melee/throw attacks have visible warnings. Thrower launches an actual swept
  projectile at the initially locked target; players can dodge and walls block
  it. Hits interrupt windup. Thief/runner carry the actual collectible, disable
  its pickup overlap, and drop it on stun or after a bounded chase. A thief
  waits eight seconds before stealing again, so a dropped acorn stays available.
- Correct cell centring, screen-centre camera ray, legal fire cadence, pickup
  one-shot latches and recoil returning to its idle position.
- Full strafe movement and vertical look. Three independently owned touch IDs
  support simultaneous movement, look and held fire; releasing/pausing clears
  inputs. Releasing movement leaves an independently held fire touch active.
  Compact objective HUD, exploring minimap, pause/resume/restart/menu,
  defeat retry and responsive layouts.
- Scene-owned spatial SFX pool, grass/gravel footsteps, original offline
  synthesized wind/water, gate/throw/warning sounds. Shared GameState volume
  and mute settings apply; muted ambience stops playback. Music loops softly.
- New audio is in assets/level1/audio; reproduce it using
  tools/generate_park_audio.py (NumPy and ffmpeg). No DSP/Python runs on Android.
