## Park and campaign

- Four connected park episodes: entrance oak, moonlit pond/gazebo, overgrown
  stone courtyard, golden oak and the parked original 240SX.
- Authored 48 x 36 grid, 1.8 m cells, 585 blocked cells. Three actual locked
  bottlenecks prevent skipping the corresponding key. Winding gravel ribbon,
  hedges, trees, benches, signs, hanging lanterns, fireflies and an optional
  one-use healing/ammo chest.
- `tools/generate_level1_layout.py` regenerates LevelData, actor positions and
  the collision scene deterministically. Python is an offline authoring tool.
- Environment uses chunked MultiMeshes, shared resources, actual lit grass,
  generated original ground texture, animated pond, blue night fog and warm
  local lights. Collisions/navigation use the same authored grid.
- 6 acorns and 3 keys. All gates must open. The final pickup preserves player
  control; entering ExitCar/BoardingArea fades out and emits the existing
  campaign completion fact. SceneFlow transitions safely outside physics flush.
- The parked car reuses Race240SXRig with duplicated materials. Level 2 source
  geometry/materials and driving logic are retained.

## Actors, input and sound

- All five archetypes use the existing shared rigged squirrel. Tank armour,
  thief satchel and runner scarf help distinguish them. Physical capsules,
  navigation routes, throttled perception and real line of sight.
- Important navigation fix: mesh paths are on the ground, actor origins at
  waist height. `path_height_offset = -ground_y` aligns reach checks without
  making waypoint tolerance large enough to cut corners.
- Melee/throw attacks have visible warnings. Thrower launches an actual swept
  projectile at the initially locked target; players can dodge and walls block
  it. Hits interrupt windup. Thief/runner carry the actual collectible, disable
  its pickup overlap, and drop it on stun or after a bounded chase.
- Correct cell centring, screen-centre camera ray, legal fire cadence, pickup
  one-shot latches and recoil returning to its idle position.
- Full strafe movement and vertical look. Three independently owned touch IDs
  support simultaneous movement, look and held fire; releasing/pausing clears
  inputs. Compact objective HUD, exploring minimap, pause/resume/restart/menu,
  defeat retry and responsive layouts.
- Scene-owned spatial SFX pool, grass/gravel footsteps, original offline
  synthesized wind/water, gate/throw/warning sounds. Shared GameState volume
  and mute settings apply; muted ambience stops playback. Music loops softly.
- New audio is in assets/level1/audio; reproduce it using
  tools/generate_park_audio.py (NumPy and ffmpeg). No DSP/Python runs on Android.
