## Level 3 result

- A coherent 48 × 32 top-down nighttime diner: entrance/takeaway counter,
  warm dining room, wood-floored bar/arcade, kitchen, cold storage and staff
  room. 54 supplied furniture placements, 113 wall runs, eight real doorways,
  supplied actor/vehicle/window/lamp/food/arcade/jukebox assets. Original asset
  files are retained. Animated variants no longer change furniture shape.
- Fixed the black floor: the old opaque fallback renderer obscured the authored
  floor. The fallback is hidden below the new floor. Stable brick/tile wall
  finishes, coordinated room palettes, contact shadows, signs, window glow,
  14 shared-texture shadowless local lights and exterior rain/reflections.
- Immutable floor/wall drawing is baked by native Godot into a 1088 × 782
  texture. Live lighting, doors and physical geometry are separate. Native
  frame counters fell from about 4700–4900 to 82–118 for desktop gameplay
  views; mobile controls used 152, full overview 223. These are software-GL
  counters and do not establish Android FPS.
- One shared authored plan drives visual placements, static collisions and
  navigation. A 12-unit AStar grid is sampled against actual body-sized physics
  geometry; doors are excluded from static planning and opened locally.
  All ten guards, eight supplies and the order are reachable.
- Ten enemies with throttled sight, remembered last-seen positions, sound
  investigation, local door use and paths around furniture. Gunshots have a
  visible windup and locked aim before a swept projectile; melee also warns
  and checks distance, facing and real line of sight. Enemies/corpses use the
  supplied top-down art and true aim rotation.
- Clearing the guards reveals the actual pizza order in the kitchen. The last
  kill keeps player control. ACTION collects the order, then the entrance
  ACTION fades and emits the existing level_completed(level3) fact; SceneFlow
  marks completion and loads the menu. Defeat offers retry/menu; pause and
  focus loss clear controls rather than silently restarting the scene.
- Responsive compact HUD, exploring minimap, world reticle, two independent
  sticks plus held FIRE, ACTION/THROW edges, sprint and weapon cycling. ACTION
  remains pending until a physics tick consumes it; render ticks cannot erase
  a brief press. Desktop uses WASD, mouse/LMB, E, Q, Shift, Tab and Esc.
- Player starts with pistol/12 rounds and a bottle. Authored ammo/medkits are
  automatic pickups; bat/shotgun/bottles use ACTION. Inventory cycling retains
  per-weapon ammo. Pickup spawns were checked against real static colliders.
- Original offline synthesized spatial rain, fridge, radio loop and eight
  combat/interaction sounds. Shared music/SFX preferences apply. Scene teardown
  stops/releases its players. Provenance and regeneration: assets/level3/LATE_SHIFT.md.
- Cached immutable sprite frames/atlas resources; 24 primed projectiles with
  hard cap 64, 96 reusable effect slots, 12 transient audio voices and 360
  permanent blood stamps. No new node/resource allocation per shot effect.
  Generic ObjectPool retains its old unlimited default for other callers.
