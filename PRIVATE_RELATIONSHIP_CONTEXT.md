# 6. HOW ACORN HUNTER EMERGED

ACORN HUNTER grew out of a real joke / personal mythology rather than a conventional game-design brief.

The central premise is deliberately stupid in the best possible way:

Darina needs a craft/project for tomorrow.

That means acorns are needed.

Carolina has to find acorns.

The acorn search becomes an "operation".

Squirrels interfere.

The squirrels escalate into an organized threat.

Carolina is eventually chased into a blue Oka.

The story abruptly becomes a racing game.

Then the entire sequence is revealed to have been a nightmare — except the squirrels are still outside the window.

This escalation is the heart of the joke.

# 18. DARINA DOORWAY BUG HISTORY

Several iterations had Darina appearing beside the computer chair rather than emerging from the doorway.

The first attempted fix simply changed Darina's coordinates. It did not solve the underlying problem.

The actual problem was the Godot Control coordinate/layout system and clipping.

A doorway mask was introduced with `clip_contents=true`.

Another bug occurred because the mask's `position` was being assigned even though the node was laid out using offsets. The corrected implementation manipulates the mask offsets directly.

This history is important because a future assistant should not repeat the old coordinate-only fix.
