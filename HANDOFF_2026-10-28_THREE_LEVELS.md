# Android 1.46 — three-level campaign update

The owner's 28 October request supersedes the former daytime garden and peaceful
route, the immediate-combat diner start, and the pursuit's three-rival-only setup.
Keep branch `fix/level2-final-game-flow`, draft PR 11; do not merge automatically.
Godot remains 4.7.2 and the production renderer remains Compatibility. Android is
1.46 / code 47, with the existing package and signing identity.

## Level 1

Darina needs six acorns for a craft. Carolina takes the squirrels' winter stock;
do not rewrite this as squirrels stealing acorns that already belonged to her.
Three service items replace the unexplained botanical specimens: pump fuse,
drive gear and electrical-panel key. The items must be installed at the pump
panel. Valves feed visible stone irrigation channels; hydraulic pressure raises
the physical greenhouse gate. The guardian must be defeated before taking the
six acorns. There is no scout-guide, stealth control, peaceful guardian bargain
or nighttime solar-mirror puzzle.

The raised gate has a saved latch, so turning water off later cannot relock the
completed objective. Completed legacy saves keep their earned reward and an
open gate; an unfinished legacy save inside the new enclosure resumes just
outside its entrance. Both migrations are checked using actual player movement.

The garden retains its architecture, textures and reconstructed foliage, with
late-evening sky, moon, fog and warm local lamps. A physical perimeter and large
landscape apron cover the former exposed map boundary. The held weapon is a
cone-gun. Evening lighting uses an offline derivative of the original bake:
`assets/level1/botanical/lighting/garden_evening.lmbake`, generated by
`tools/prepare_botanical_evening.gd`; original source lighting remains available.
The transformation and unchanged probe/receiver structure are recorded in its
provenance. This is not a claim of a new physically traced night bake.
No expensive SSAO or volumetric fog is enabled for the phone.
The evening Compatibility grade uses its own contrast value; the former daylight
grade lifted near-black surfaces into grey. Keep the cross-renderer exposure
limits unchanged when checking this presentation.

Normal and alternate-route playthroughs both require the real two-phase fight;
their service-item/valve order differs. The new evening test covers installed
parts, water, closed/open hydraulic collision, weapon and boundary views.
Geometry fidelity comparisons retain their original limits using the current
shared evening presentation. The old sunny screenshots are historical references,
not the authorized appearance for this release. Actual P60 FPS remains unmeasured.

## Level 2

Oncoming civilian Camrys travel in the opposite direction and participate in
swept physical contacts. Roadblocks use transverse Camrys, warning lights and
readable openings; vehicles can be hit and displaced. Traffic and pursuers must
respect those openings, including a pursuer still approaching after Carolina
has passed. Helicopter attacks are separated from ground obstructions.

Pursuers keep pressure, flank and brake-check more often. Their small engine
advantage is applied inside the physical powertrain; Carolina's 219 km/h limit
and steering/traction contracts remain. Do not implement visible position warps.
A same-input 90-second baseline/current comparison measures real nearby time
and contacts, not parameter differences alone. The physical full-route driver
avoids actual traffic/openings while retaining the former timing/offroad bounds.

The renderer builds and clips road/scenery for both camera directions, retaining
rear surroundings through strong orbit and sector boundaries. Finite sky pan
and appropriate tunnel-side UVs accompany the rear projection correction.
Native views, including bridge and tunnel structures, require visual acceptance;
geometry counters alone are not evidence that a submitted mesh is visible.

## Level 3

The mission begins outside the diner. Carolina enters, calls at an unanswered
reception, then talks to the cook in the kitchen about her pizza. The cook notices
the cone-gun on her back and jokes about a squirrel in a wheel. Original pixel
portraits have talking mouths, blinking, typewriter text and separate reveal /
advance input. The modal UI releases gameplay controls and deduplicates a raw
finger and its emulated mouse event. Kitchen and aftermath lines are separate.

After dialogue the camera follows a drifting van with engine/tire sounds and
persistent skid marks. The existing clean thirteen-frame van-door strip is used;
the legacy sixty-six-frame strip contains a human and must not be used. The van
collider leaves a real opening: ten armed squirrels physically leave its door
and move to window/door stacks. A breakable glass collider opens a breach;
a flashbang enters the adjacent room before the assault. No guards occupy the
interior before this arrival.

After defeating all ten, Carolina returns to the shocked cook, takes the pizza,
leaves and boards the matching topdown pixel 240. Its driver door opens on
approach and boarding is automatic. The visible cone-gun sling remains while
existing pistol/melee combat works. Positional sound follows the cinematic
camera during the raid and returns to the player afterwards.

`level3_story.gd` owns phases from street through boarding. `begin_raid()` runs
actual arrival; the production Skip commits a consistent final raid state once.
The full playthrough uses real player input, with no teleport, health/ammo or
progress override. Unit/render fixtures run the same raid before freezing actors;
they may suppress preparatory GPU draws, restoring the render-loop flag before
captures. They are explicitly staged fixtures, not a recorded full playthrough.

Raw portrait and 240 authoring sources remain excluded from Android. The font's
upstream licence is included. Runtime assets are the prepared pixel strips,
vehicle body/door, and embedded Cyrillic font. Private original portraits are
not added to the repository or APK.
